# ArchLabs

**Intelligence that never leaves.**

We build specialist AI agents that run where your systems run, act only within the limits you set, and make your operations more reliable every time they work.

The best agent for your infrastructure is not the biggest model. It is the one trained for the job.

## What we build on

- **Small by design.** Specialist models trained for one job, not one general model prompted for everything.
- **Private.** Telemetry, logs and command output stay inside your environment.
- **Sovereign.** Runs on your infrastructure, under your control.
- **Governed.** Every action sits inside an envelope you define. Anything beyond goes to a person.
- **Efficient.** Small enough to run where you already run.

## Where we start

SRE first, not SRE only: site reliability, then observability, platform operations and security operations. Every domain where data cannot leave.

We are opening to a small number of engineering teams first. If your systems are too important to send outside, we would like to hear from you.
